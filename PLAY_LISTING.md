## Full description (≤ 4000 chars)
Locus turns the room you're in into a place to meet people.

At a concert, a party, a festival, a campus, or anywhere a crowd gathers, Locus shows you the people nearby — and it works with no internet at all. Your phone talks directly to other phones over a Bluetooth mesh, so you can discover, connect, and chat even when the cell network is dead and there's no Wi-Fi in sight.

HOW IT WORKS
• Make your card — a name, an emoji, a one-liner, your vibes, and what you're here for. No account, no phone number, no email.
• Discover — swipe through cards of people actually around you. Right to connect, left to pass.
• Connect — when you both tap connect, it's a match. Nobody else ever sees who liked whom.
• Chat — your conversations are end-to-end encrypted, phone to phone.

NO INTERNET, NO SERVER, NO SIGN-UP
Locus runs on an offline Bluetooth mesh. There are no accounts and nothing to log into. Your identity is a key that lives only on your device.

BUILT FOR WHERE YOU ALREADY ARE
Dead signal at the venue? Airplane mode crowd? Locus doesn't care. If people around you have it, you can find each other.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
• No account, no phone number, no email.
• Your location is never recorded or shared — proximity is just radio range.
• Likes, matches, and chats stay on your device or travel encrypted between phones.
• Block and report anyone, instantly.

Locus is for adults (18+).

## Notes for the Data Safety form (must match the privacy policy)
Data collected/shared when online:
- Name (optional display name on the card)
- Approximate age (optional)
- User-provided profile text (bio, vibes, intent)
- App activity: a "last seen" timestamp
- An anonymous authentication identifier
NOT collected: precise/coarse location, contacts, phone number, email, messages, likes, matches.
All optional profile data is user-entered. Data is encrypted in transit. Users can request deletion.
